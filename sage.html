Wire surface

A small HTTP surface with an explicit caller.

The quickstart lists unauthenticated GET /ready, authenticated POST /decide and authenticated POST /decide/batch. The OpenAPI edition also describes model and usage-estimate routes. Every decision call needs an API key; this site makes none.

Five answer shapes

Choose the shape before asking the question.

yesno

Binary call

Returns a Yes probability, a Yes/No answer and decisiveness away from a tie.

Vendor-reportedL-KINDS
choice

One option

Chooses an argmax from a caller-defined set and returns per-option probabilities that need not sum to one.

Vendor-reportedL-KINDS
scale

Five-level rubric

Uses exactly five caller-described levels, zero through four, and returns an expected level plus confidence.

Vendor-reportedL-KINDS
sort

Ordered list

Ranks a list by the written criterion and returns one list-level confidence for the order.

Vendor-reportedL-KINDS
tags

Zero or more labels

Applies any matching labels from a caller-defined set, with confidence attached to returned tags.

Vendor-reportedL-KINDS
batch

Several decisions

The batch route accepts several decision requests and can expose item-level outcomes rather than turning one item into a claim about all.

Confidence

Decisive is not the same as correct.

For Yes/No, the published confidence is distance from a 50/50 tie. Sage returns a decision signal on success and does not abstain. Levanto leaves automation, review and escalation thresholds to the application.

Vendor-reported L-KINDS L-ERRORS
No universal threshold is established here. A confidence number does not supply calibration for Shoggoth packets, a false-refusal cost or a safe consequence level. Those remain unknown until a bounded pilot measures them.

Failure and data

The operational contract matters as much as the answer.

400 · 401 · 402 · 503

Published errors distinguish invalid input, missing or invalid authentication, insufficient balance and a loading or unavailable service.

Vendor-reportedL-ERRORS

Ordinary zero retention; diagnostic exception

Levanto's policy says ordinary inference inputs and outputs are not retained, while failed or abnormally slow requests may retain full redacted payloads for 30 days.

Vendor-reportedL-PRIVACY

Probabilistic output

Levanto's terms say outputs may be wrong, incomplete, biased or unsuitable, and place evaluation, oversight and downstream action on the customer.
