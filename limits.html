Eight boundaries

Failure stays visible, local and recoverable.

Authority inversion

A useful semantic answer is mistaken for proof that a Promise Machine gate passed. Control: preserve separate evidence classes and never return permission from the adapter.

Proposed control

Confidence misread

Yes/No confidence is published as decisiveness away from a tie, not correctness for Shoggoth's packet distribution.

Control: measure errors by policy and consequence.

Service failure

The public contract names 400, 401, 402 and 503 states.

Control: every non-success or timeout refuses the dependent semantic route without bypassing the existing path.

Diagnostic retention

Ordinary inference is described as zero-retention, but failed or slow requests may retain redacted full payloads for 30 days.

Control: classify and minimise every field as though that exception may apply.

Credential leak

A key reaches browser code, a URL, a command argument, a fixture or a diagnostic. Control: keep credentials in a separately authorised server process and reject credential-shaped packet content.

Schema or model drift

The service changes an accepted field, model id or answer shape. Control: closed parsing, pinned contract digests and policy expiry instead of permissive fallback.

Source drift

Levanto's web docs are mutable while Shoggoth's current contract is commit-pinned. Control: record observation dates and digests, then force claim review when an edition changes.

Vendor benchmark

A useful record with unlike clocks.

Levanto's dated Sage-versus-Claude benchmark is vendor-run. Its Sage model latency excludes network while the Claude wall-clock includes network, and the tested Sage model predates the current OpenAPI default. This edition draws no neutral latency result or service-level promise from it.

Cost and capacity

No cost forecast without a packet distribution.

Public pricing and limits can describe current commercial rules. They cannot establish this design's call volume, review cost, timeout budget or acceptable failure rate. Those remain unknown until the pilot fixes a workload.

Failure posture

When the semantic route fails, the system loses that advice—not its evidence, inspection path or human recovery.

Proposed

Before any live call

  • Accept the exact packet, policy and response schemas.
  • Approve data classes, redaction, retention assumptions and destination.
  • Define timeout, retry and every documented error branch.
  • Measure false review and false refusal on versioned fixtures.
  • Choose who can suspend, rotate and revoke the adapter.
  • Keep the Promise Machine and controller gates unchanged.
