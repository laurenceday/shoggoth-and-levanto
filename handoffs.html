Current packet rule

A hand-off carries less than the whole conversation.

Fiat workers receive source-bound briefs for one role and one phase. The packet names the branch, runbook step, evidence boundary and exact output owed; the controller keeps the durable state and receipt sequence.

Candidate checkpoints

Three seams worth testing, in conservative order.

01 · Research

Does this source need human review?

Sage may flag a public document or mutable vendor claim for extra review. It cannot promote that material into a stronger evidence class.

Proposed
02 · Audit

Which finding deserves attention first?

Sage may sort or tag already-recorded findings for a reviewer. It cannot lower severity, close a finding or replace the clean-round rule.

Proposed
03 · Publication

Should this packet pause?

In the first pilot, Sage may request review or refusal before publication. It cannot remove a deterministic gate or permit the merge.

Proposed

Never cross

No secret, no full context dump, no implied permission.

A future adapter refuses packets with undeclared fields, credentials, private filesystem paths, oversized content, missing policy digests or a destination outside its allowlist. Refusal leaves the original Shoggoth inspection and human-review routes available.
