Policy proposal

Two contracts

Keep the semantic question separate from the consequence rule.

The Sage question asks what the content appears to mean. Local policy asks whether that result is usable here, at this confidence, for this consequence, under this source edition. Only the second contract chooses the route.

Policy edition

Six things that cannot be supplied at runtime.

01

Stable id and digest

The packet names an accepted policy edition; it cannot send new policy prose and call it approved.

Proposed
02

Closed question

The decision kind, instructions and option or rubric definitions are fixed and reviewable.

Proposed
03

Allowed fields

Only declared, redacted content classes may leave the process; every other field is refused.

Proposed
04

Response contract

The accepted model, schema, status codes and metadata fields are closed rather than best-effort parsed.

Proposed
05

Route rule

Threshold bands map to review, refusal or an explicitly low-consequence route in deterministic code.

Proposed
06

Expiry and recovery

Source drift, model drift or a failed check expires the policy and leaves replay and human review available.

Thresholds

Pick no number before measuring the errors.

This study does not know a safe universal confidence band. The pilot must record disagreement, false review, false refusal, service failure and consequence before a threshold can be accepted for one question and one packet distribution.
