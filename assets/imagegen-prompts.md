## generated/cooperative-threshold.webp

Role: original hero artwork for the cooperative Shoggoth and Levanto research
site.

Production prompt:

> Create an original, cinematic vertical editorial illustration for a
> cooperative AI-safety architecture site. At dawn in a quiet desert landscape,
> a lean silver geometric wildcat figure stands beside a monumental white
> threshold arch. The wildcat calmly brings together two distinct luminous
> streams, one electric blue and one warm gold. The streams meet and continue
> through the arch without losing their colours. Include still reflective
> water, distant mesas and one sculptural windswept tree. The mood is
> thoughtful, precise and hopeful rather than triumphant. Use restrained
> modernist forms, soft sunrise light, muted mineral colours and fine cinematic
> texture. No words, logos, badges, UI, extra characters or copied composition.

Reference roles:

- The supplied mascot kit informed only the Wildcat's lean geometric
  proportions, angular ears, silver planes and yellow-eyed expression. No kit
  file or source image is present in this repository.
- The supplied public-conversation screenshot and Levanto's public dawn motif
  informed only the warm-light, arch, landscape and reflection vocabulary.
  The final scene does not reproduce a Levanto logo, exact homepage image or
  page layout.
- The cooperative architecture supplied the blue-and-gold streams: distinct
  inputs meeting at a threshold while retaining separate roles.

Exclusions: no text, logo, trademark lock-up, product endorsement, combat,
winner/loser framing, private source image or photorealistic person.

- Public derivative: WebP, 1122 × 1402, 74,006 bytes.
- Compression: cwebp, quality 76, method 6, metadata removed.

  bd04c8eb0af1104811acc62763c6245098d3b9ad66961986315b50718f772377.
- Responsive use: intrinsic width and height in HTML, CSS height: auto
